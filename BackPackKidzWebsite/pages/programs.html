Programs & How It Works

Simple steps. Steady results.

Back Pack Kidz turns local generosity into weekend food for children. The process is direct and repeatable: neighbors give, volunteers pack, schools distribute, and kids return ready to learn.

How your help moves through the community

Four steps connect a neighbor's gift to a child's weekend.

01

Neighbors give

What we provide

Back Pack Kidz supports children at every grade level, plus the partnerships that hold it all together.

01

Weekend food bags

Elementary students receive bags of sealed, easy-open food to carry them through Saturday and Sunday.

02

School pantry support

Middle and high school pantries give older students discreet access to food when they need it.

03

Hygiene & product support

Basic hygiene items help students feel cared for and ready to show up with confidence.

04

Community partnerships

Schools, churches, businesses, and civic groups extend the reach of weekend food support across the county.

Food donation guidelines

Because bags travel home with children and are opened without adult help, the food we collect needs to be safe, simple, and kid-friendly. Please keep these guidelines in mind when you donate.

Great items to donate

  • Single-portion, sealed, non-perishable foods
  • Easy to open with no can opener or utensils needed
  • Shelf-stable — no heating or refrigeration required
  • Fruit cups and applesauce cups
  • Cereal bowls and crackers
  • Pudding cups and shelf-stable milk or juice
  • Easy-open canned meals such as pasta

Please avoid

  • Peanut butter, nuts, and other common allergens
  • Glass jars or containers that can break
  • Items that need a stove, microwave, or fridge
  • Family-size or bulk packaging
  • Expired or opened products
  • Candy or low-nutrition snack food as a main item

100% of your donation goes directly to helping hungry children.
